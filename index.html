About the name

Bento 弁当

Bento (弁当) means “boxed meal” or “lunchbox” in Japanese. Just like a bento brings different little dishes together in one organized box, Bento brings your recipes, meal plans, grocery lists, and food ideas together in one place—making everyday meals easier to plan and enjoy.
